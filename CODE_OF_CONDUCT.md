# Code of conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to a respectful, harassment-free environment for everyone,
regardless of background, identity, experience, or professional status.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Critique methods, evidence, and code rather than people.
- Welcome correction, especially where scientific claims exceed the data.
- Protect private or sensitive genomic information.
- Disclose uncertainty, conflicts, and limitations honestly.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, deliberate scientific
misrepresentation, and publication of private genomic data are unacceptable.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner through the contact
method on the owner's GitHub profile. Reports will be reviewed promptly and
confidentially. Maintainers may edit or remove contributions or restrict
participation when necessary to protect the community.
